# CalculatesAll

> Free browser-based online calculators and everyday utility tools for money, mathematics, dates, health screening, unit conversion and Tasbih counting.

**Public website brand:** CalculatesAll  
**Official website:** https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/  
**Source repository/project name:** CalculateAllInOne  
**Maintainer:** Muhammad Fayyaz Adeel

CalculatesAll is an independently maintained collection of free online calculators. Visitors can use the tools in a browser without creating an account. Each calculator page describes its supported inputs, calculation method, examples where relevant, and important limitations. Calculator entries are processed in the browser rather than submitted to a calculation API.

## Calculator directory

### Money and business

- [EMI calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/emi-calculator/) — estimates fixed-rate monthly instalments and provides a repayment schedule.
- [Zakat calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/zakat-calculator/) — supports the stated Sunni Hanafi method and the limited Ja'fari trading-goods method published by Sayyid Sistani. Users enter their own metal prices; the tool is not a religious ruling for every school or circumstance.
- [Profit and loss calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/profit-loss-calculator/) — calculates product profit or loss and business operating results from the values entered.
- [Discount calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/discount-calculator/) — calculates a discount amount and reduced price.
- [Compound interest calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/compound-interest-calculator/) — models growth from a starting balance, nominal annual rate, duration and compounding frequency.

### Mathematics and conversions

- [Standard and scientific calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/standard-calculator/) — everyday arithmetic and supported scientific operations.
- [Percentage calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/percentage-calculator/) — percentage of a number, percentage share, percentage change and reverse-percentage calculations.
- [Percentage difference calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/percentage-difference-calculator/) — compares two values using the absolute difference divided by their average.
- [Ratio calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/ratio-calculator/) — simplifies ratios, scales equivalent ratios and solves supported proportions.
- [Fraction calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/fraction-calculator/) — adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides fractions.
- [Unit converter](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/unit-converter/) — converts supported weight, length and temperature units using stated factors.

### Dates and time

- [Age calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/age-calculator/) — calculates age in Gregorian years, months and days.
- [Years to days calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/years-to-days-calculator/) — distinguishes fixed-year estimates, Gregorian-average conversions and exact calendar-year calculations.
- [Date difference calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/date-calculator/) — calculates elapsed or inclusive Gregorian calendar days between dates.
- [Online stopwatch](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/stopwatch/) — browser-based elapsed-time tool.
- [Time and date query calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/query-calculator/) — answers defined time-unit, date and simple arithmetic question patterns. It is not a general-purpose AI assistant or web search engine, and unsupported questions are rejected rather than guessed.

### Health and worship tools

- [Adult BMI calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/bmi-calculator/) — provides an adult BMI screening result, not a medical diagnosis.
- [Adult calorie calculator](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/calorie-calculator/) — estimates adult maintenance energy using stated equations and assumptions; it is not a medical prescription.
- [Tasbih counter](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/tasbih-counter/) — counts deliberate taps for selected or custom dhikr phrases and targets. It does not listen to or verify recitation.

## Accuracy, privacy and limitations

- Review the formula, units, assumptions and exclusions on the individual calculator page.
- Results are general calculation aids. They are not professional medical, financial, tax, legal or religious advice.
- BMI is an adult screening measure, not a diagnosis. Calorie results are estimates, not individual dietary prescriptions.
- Zakat calculations follow only the method and scope stated on the Zakat page. Scholarly rulings can differ.
- Calculator inputs are processed in the browser and are not sent to a calculation API. The website's Privacy Policy explains ordinary technical data and any third-party resources.
- Report a reproducible calculation or content issue through the [Contact page](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/contact/).

## Project identity

CalculatesAll is the public website name. CalculateAllInOne is the source-project and repository name. The public website is the canonical destination for current calculator availability, methods, limitations and policies.
