# CalculateAllInOne

Free, browser-based calculators for everyday calculations across health, money, mathematics, dates, time, and worship-related needs.

**Website:** https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/

## What it offers

CalculateAllInOne brings practical calculators together in one place, including:

- Health tools such as BMI and adult calorie calculations
- Money tools such as EMI, compound interest, discount, profit and loss, and Zakat
- Mathematics tools such as percentage and fraction calculations
- Date and time utilities, including date calculations, stopwatch, and unit conversion

## Product principles

- Clear inputs and readable results
- Explainable calculation methods where provided
- Browser-based access without requiring a paid subscription

For the live calculators and current tool availability, visit the [CalculateAllInOne website](https://calculateallinone.vercel.app/).
